Acme Retail Inc. 2025 Annual Report

MD&A
Revenue increased to $700 million and gross margin improved to 35.0 percent as supply chain conditions normalized and we expanded our private-label assortment. Operating income also rose, reflecting lower logistics costs and improved productivity. We believe the business remains well positioned for continued expansion.

Liquidity and capital structure
Working capital improved to $170 million, supported by higher cash generation and a moderate reduction in leverage. Inventory levels increased steadily but were well controlled relative to sales growth.
