Acme Retail Inc. 2023 Annual Report

MD&A
We delivered solid demand across our store base in 2023, and we maintained a disciplined approach to pricing and promotions. Gross margins were 33.8 percent, supported by favorable product mix and a steady cost base. Operating income improved as we focused on productivity and better inventory turns.

Liquidity and capital structure
Current assets were $410 million and current liabilities were $280 million, giving us a healthy liquidity position. Working capital remained strong even as we continued to invest in inventory.
