Acme Retail Inc. 2024 Annual Report

MD&A
Gross margin was 34.0 percent in 2024, down from 35.2 percent in 2023, as higher freight and markdown activity offset pricing improvements. We also experienced a modest increase in inventory during the year, which contributed to operating costs and tighter liquidity. Net income was $52 million, reflecting careful expense management.

Liquidity and capital structure
Current assets increased to $450 million while current liabilities were $310 million. Current ratio remained stable, and we continued to monitor debt and equity levels as we funded growth.
