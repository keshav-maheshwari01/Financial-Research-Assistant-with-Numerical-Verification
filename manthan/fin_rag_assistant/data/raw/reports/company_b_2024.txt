Northwind Goods 2024 Annual Report

MD&A
Revenue increased to $890 million, and gross margin improved to 32.6 percent as channel mix strengthened. Operating income rose as we simplified the assortment and increased productivity. We continue to focus on balancing inventory investments with margin stability.

Liquidity and capital structure
Current assets were $660 million and current liabilities were $390 million, leaving a solid operating buffer. Inventory was a meaningful component of current assets, but the ratio remained manageable.
