Northwind Goods 2025 Annual Report

MD&A
Gross margin was 33.3 percent in 2025, supported by margin expansion and disciplined promotions. Revenue increased again to $960 million, with operating income reaching $132 million. Our teams remained focused on inventory productivity and disciplined spending.

Liquidity and capital structure
We ended the year with $720 million in current assets and $410 million in current liabilities, which supported continued investment and financial flexibility.
